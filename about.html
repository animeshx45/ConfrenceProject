Conference Scope & Vision

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence for Computational Science and Engineering (ICAICSE 2026) is organized by the National Institute of Technology Srinagar. The conference emphasises high-quality, original and interdisciplinary research where artificial intelligence enhances classical computational methods, scientific simulation, engineering design, prediction and numerical optimization.

As computational simulations in aerodynamics, materials, chemical processes, and structural engineering grow in scale, traditional numerical algorithms are increasingly complemented by physics-informed neural networks, generative AI, and high-performance computing pipelines. ICAICSE 2026 bridges these paradigms to cultivate transformative scientific discovery.

Core Objectives of ICAICSE 2026

01

Multidisciplinary Platform

A platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers and industry professionals working at the intersection of AI, machine learning, scientific computing, computational mathematics, simulation and engineering.

02

AI as a Computational Partner

Promoting AI not merely as an empirical data-analysis tool, but as a foundational partner for scientific discovery, prediction, simulation, optimization and engineering innovation.

03

A Specialised Forum

A dedicated forum for research in which AI enhances computational methods, scientific simulation, engineering design, surrogate modeling, and multi-scale optimization.

04

A Global, Interdisciplinary Community

Bringing AI researchers together with domain scientists and computational engineers to solve real-world challenges across energy, materials, healthcare, and infrastructure.

ORGANIZING INSTITUTION

National Institute of Technology Srinagar

Established in 1960 as Regional Engineering College Srinagar and elevated to an Institute of National Importance, NIT Srinagar is located on the western banks of Dal Lake near the Hazratbal shrine. The institute is a premier destination for higher engineering pedagogy, state-of-the-art computational laboratories, and pioneering research across engineering disciplines.
