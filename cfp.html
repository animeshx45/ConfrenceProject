Submission Policies & Integrity Guidelines

  • ✓ Originality: Submissions must be original and not currently under review by another conference or journal.
  • ✓ Plagiarism Screening: All manuscripts undergo automated similarity and plagiarism detection. Unethical submissions will be rejected immediately.
  • ✓ Double-Blind Anonymity: Authors must remove author names, affiliations, and identifying self-citations from initial review drafts.
  • ✓ Best Paper Awards: Outstanding submissions across tracks are eligible for Best Research Paper Awards sponsored by J&K Bank.
