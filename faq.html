Answers & Evaluation Criteria.

Everything you need to know about peer-review criteria, submission policies, recommendations, and frequently asked questions for ICAICSE 2026.

EVALUATION METRICS

The 10 Official Review Criteria

All submitted research undergoes rigorous double-blind assessment by international specialists based on merit, originality, and computational depth.

01

Relevance to Conference

Alignment with AI, scientific computing, mathematical modeling, simulation, and engineering problems.

02

Originality & Novelty

Novelty in the proposed methodology, algorithm, computational framework, or innovative application.

03

Technical Quality & Soundness

Theoretical rigor, mathematical soundness, experimental validity, and error quantification.

04

AI / Computational Contribution

Significance of AI methods or computational algorithms for solving the targeted engineering challenge.

05

Validation & Benchmarking

Adequacy of baseline comparisons, benchmark datasets, computational convergence, and empirical metrics.

06

Results & Discussion

Clarity, depth, and thorough interpretation of results compared against existing computational methods.

07

Reproducibility

Sufficient technical detail regarding algorithms, parameters, datasets, and execution settings.

08

Clarity & Organization

High quality of manuscript writing, logical structure, clarity of figures, tables, and references.

09

Significance & Potential Impact

Practical value and long-term implications for computational science, industry, or domain engineering.

10

Responsible & Ethical AI

Considerations of explainability, transparency, environmental cost of compute, and safety constraints.

Official Review Decision Categories

Submitted papers receive one of the following recommendations after double-blind review:

Strong Accept Accept Weak Accept Borderline Weak Reject Reject
HELP & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding conference scope, submission protocols, review criteria, and dates.

ICAICSE is the International Conference on Artificial Intelligence for Computational Science and Engineering, organized by the National Institute of Technology Srinagar. ICAICSE 2026 is the inaugural edition uniting research at the confluence of AI and scientific simulation.

Researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers and industry professionals. Participation from academia, research laboratories, industry, and international institutions is warmly welcomed.

Scientific machine learning, physics-informed AI, AI-driven simulation and digital twins, AI-HPC, computational modelling and optimization, uncertainty quantification, generative AI for science, and AI applications in energy, healthcare, agriculture, climate, materials and engineering.

The submission deadline is 15 December 2026. Author guidelines, manuscript templates, and the submission portal link will be announced. This website does not accept paper uploads directly.

Paper Submission: 15 December 2026 · Acceptance Notification: 25 December 2026 · Final Camera-Ready Papers: 31 December 2026 · Conference Dates: 20–21 October 2026.

Double-blind peer review by international experts, plus a mandatory plagiarism check, assessed strictly across ten published criteria.

In Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, India. The specific conference auditorium venue is TO BE ANNOUNCED.
