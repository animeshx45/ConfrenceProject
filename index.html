01 / ABOUT ICAICSE

An international meeting point for AI, computational science and engineering.

ICAICSE brings together work on artificial intelligence, machine learning, scientific computing, computational mathematics, simulation and engineering. It emphasises high-quality, original and interdisciplinary research, with a focus on AI that enhances computational methods, scientific simulation, engineering design, prediction and optimization.

05 / REVIEW PROCESS

A structured review process for rigorous research.

Recommendations weigh novelty, technical rigor, relevance and potential impact — not publication venue or the reputation of authors or institutions.

Review Criteria

01

Relevance to the Conference

Alignment with AI, computational science, scientific computing, modelling, simulation or engineering applications.

02

Originality and Novelty

Innovation in the proposed methodology, model, algorithm, framework or application.

03

Technical Quality and Correctness

Soundness of methodology, theory, experiments, simulations and results.

04

AI / Computational Contribution

Significance of the AI or computational techniques for the targeted problem.

05

Experimental / Computational Validation

Adequacy of datasets, benchmarks, simulations, comparisons, statistics and metrics.

06

Results and Discussion

Clarity, significance and interpretation of results, compared with existing approaches.

07

Reproducibility

Sufficient detail on algorithms, datasets and settings to enable verification.

08

Clarity and Organization

Writing, structure, figures, tables, references and presentation.

09

Significance and Impact

Potential contribution to discovery, engineering practice, methods or applications.

10

Ethical and Responsible AI

Explainability, fairness, privacy, uncertainty and safety, where applicable.

Recommendation Categories

Submitted papers receive one of the following official recommendations following double-blind assessment:

Strong Accept Accept Weak Accept Borderline Weak Reject Reject
