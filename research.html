02 / RESEARCH AREAS

8 Multidisciplinary Research Tracks.

Uniting fundamental computational theory with applied artificial intelligence. All submissions undergo double-blind peer review by international experts across scientific domains.

Featured Track
🧠

Scientific Machine Learning

Track 01 · Rigorous Theory & Practice

Machine learning methods for scientific computing and computational modelling, bridging algorithmic learning with classical scientific principles, conservation laws, and numerical analysis.

Submit to Track 01 →
Featured Track
⚛️

Physics-Informed AI

Track 02 · Fundamental & Applied

AI architectures informed by the governing physics of the problems they address, enforcing conservation laws, boundary conditions, Hamiltonian systems, and partial differential equations.

Submit to Track 02 →
🌐

AI-Driven Simulation & Digital Twins

Track 03 · High Impact Systems

AI-assisted simulation and digital twin approaches for real-time asset monitoring, prognostic health estimation, cyber-physical systems, and reduced-order surrogate modeling.

Submit to Track 03 →
⚡

AI-HPC Architectures

Track 04 · High Performance Computing

AI-enabled high-performance computing, distributed training, heterogeneous accelerator architectures (GPUs, TPUs), exascale workflows, and parallel algorithms for scientific simulation.

Submit to Track 04 →
📐

Computational Modelling & Optimization

Track 05 · Core Foundations

Computational modelling and intelligent optimization for complex engineering problems, multi-objective design, topology optimization, inverse problems, and evolutionary computation.

Submit to Track 05 →
📊

Uncertainty Quantification

Track 06 · Reliability & Trust

Uncertainty in AI-based and computational models, Bayesian deep learning, sensitivity analysis, epistemic vs. aleatoric uncertainty estimation, and probabilistic risk evaluation.

Submit to Track 06 →
🧪

Generative AI for Science

Track 07 · Emerging Frontier

Generative diffusion models and transformer architectures applied to scientific discovery, molecular synthesis, drug candidate screening, material discovery, and synthetic scientific datasets.

Submit to Track 07 →
🚀

Cross-Sector AI Applications

Track 08 · Real-World Impact

AI applications in renewable energy, healthcare imaging, smart agriculture, climate and weather modeling, structural mechanics, and materials engineering delivering measurable impact.

Submit to Track 08 →

Submission Format & Scope Note

Submissions in all tracks may present theoretical contributions, computational experiments, benchmarks, software toolkits, or industrial applications. The final detailed call for papers, formatting templates, and portal links will be published before the submission deadline of 15 December 2026.

Review the submission stages, manuscript preparation advice, and check your paper format before the deadline.
