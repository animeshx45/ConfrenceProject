09 / VENUE & LOCATION

Hosted in the Scenic Valley of Srinagar, India.

Organized by the National Institute of Technology Srinagar, situated on the picturesque western banks of Dal Lake. Experience autumn in Kashmir while participating in premier international research.

🏛️

Host Campus

National Institute of Technology Srinagar (NIT Srinagar), an institute of national importance established in 1960. Located at Hazratbal, Srinagar 190006, Jammu & Kashmir, India.

📍

Conference Halls

Specific conference auditoriums, plenary session halls, and technical track workshop venues within Srinagar will be announced in the official conference program: TO BE ANNOUNCED.

✈️

City & Travel Connectivity

Srinagar is connected via Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport (SXR) with regular direct flights to New Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and major international transit hubs. Late October in Srinagar is characterized by crisp autumn weather and vibrant Chinar foliage.

Traveler & Delegate Assistance

Airport Transfers

Prepaid taxis and airport cabs are readily available at Srinagar Airport (approx. 20 km to the NIT Srinagar campus).

Accommodations

A list of recommended hotels, institutional guest houses, and special delegate rates will be shared with registered participants.

Visa Support for International Authors

Official invitation letters for conference visa applications will be issued to accepted authors upon completion of registration.

Questions about travel or accommodations?

Contact our local organizing team for logistical support and conference guidance.
